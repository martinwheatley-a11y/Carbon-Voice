Anchored Audio Comments Setup

1. Zero Doc Body Clutter: Sending a recording from the pop-out uploads the audio to Drive and stages it at the top of the Google Docs Side Panel—nothing is injected into the student/staff document body.

2. Highlight & Link: The Side Panel prompts the staff member to highlight the target words in the Doc and click Link to Highlighted Text, binding it via a persistent Google Docs NamedRange + optional soft amber highlight.

3. Click-to-Locate & Play: Clicking any quote in the Side Panel highlights that exact sentence in the Doc and plays the voice feedback + transcript inline.
