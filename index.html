Personal No-SDK Google Docs Setup (2 Mins)

Run this privately inside your own Google Docs—no Google Cloud Console or Marketplace SDK needed.

Step 1: Open your Google Doc → click Extensions > Apps Script (no Web App deployment needed!).

Step 2: Replace Code.gs with the updated script in tab 2, and paste this updated file into Index.html. Hit Save.

Step 3: Reload your Google Doc and click Carbon Voice > Open Voice Sidebar.

Step 4: Record right here in this browser tab, click .webm, and drag the downloaded file straight into the Google Docs Sidebar — it uploads to your College Drive and inserts the link at your cursor (hover link → click Chip)!
